Privacy Policy

EDGR Life · Effective November 18, 2026

EDGR Life ("the app") is a personal Android app made for a single couple to share a calendar and notes. It is not sold, advertised or offered to the public. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and where that information goes.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • Your basic Google profile (name, email address and photo), to show who is signed in and to tell the two users apart.
  • Google Calendar, to create one calendar named "EDGR Life", share it with your partner, and read, add, edit and delete events in it.
  • Google Sheets, to create and use one spreadsheet named "EDGR Life data", which stores your names, colours and notes.
  • Google Drive (only files the app creates), to share that spreadsheet with your partner. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

The app also uses notification permission to show reminders on your phone.

How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the app's features: showing and editing the shared calendar and notes, scheduling reminders, and showing upcoming events in the home screen widget. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the information is stored

  • In your own Google account. Events live in the "EDGR Life" Google Calendar and notes and settings live in the "EDGR Life data" Google Sheet. Both are owned by the person who set the app up and shared only with their partner.
  • On your phone. The app keeps a local copy of upcoming events and notes so it works offline and can show the widget.

The app has no server of its own. Your data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. The only sharing is between the two partners, through Google's own calendar and file sharing, which you set up in the app.

App updates are delivered through Expo's update service (expo.dev). When the app checks for an update, Expo receives standard technical details such as your IP address, app version and device type, as any website would. No calendar, note or account data is sent to Expo.

Google API Services User Data Policy

EDGR Life's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not transferred to others, not used for advertising, and not read by humans, except with your consent or where needed for security or to comply with the law.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Signing out of the app deletes its local copy from your phone.
  • You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete the "EDGR Life" calendar and the "EDGR Life data" spreadsheet from Google Calendar and Google Drive whenever you like. Uninstalling the app does not delete them.

Children

The app is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
