EDGR Life

A private shared calendar and notes app for two people.

EDGR Life is a personal Android app made for one couple. It helps the two of us plan birthdays, appointments, anniversaries and reminders on a shared Google Calendar, and keep shared notes in a Google Sheet. Each event and note shows whose it is.

The app is not sold or listed in any app store. It is installed directly on our own phones.

Features

  • Shared calendar with events for either of us or both, with times, durations and repeats.
  • Reminders, with a louder alert for important events.
  • A home screen widget showing upcoming events.
  • Shared notes and checklists.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. In short: your data stays in your own Google account and on your phone. There are no servers, ads or tracking.
